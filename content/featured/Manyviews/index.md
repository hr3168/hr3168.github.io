abstract: >
  ManyViews is an AI-assisted tool designed to enhance citizen engagement in urban design by enabling non-experts to envision and contribute design proposals through intuitive visual transformations and collaborative visualization.
background: >
  <p>Urban planning and design traditionally rely on professional architectural drawings that are difficult for non-experts to interpret and engage with. This creates a significant barrier to meaningful citizen participation in the design of their urban spaces. Research shows that citizens struggle with two key challenges: <strong>spatial understanding</strong> of technical drawings and <strong>intent expression</strong> when trying to communicate their design ideas.</p><p>To address these challenges, we developed ManyViews, a tool that democratizes urban design participation by leveraging generative AI to bridge the gap between professional architectural representations and citizen understanding.</p>
methodology: >
  <p>ManyViews employs a <strong>three-stage pipeline</strong> to facilitate citizen engagement in urban design:</p><ul><li><strong>View Transformation:</strong> Uses generative AI to automatically convert professional architectural drawings (such as plans, sections, and elevations) into human-perspective renderings that are more intuitive for non-experts to understand. This stage leverages state-of-the-art diffusion models to create realistic visualizations from technical drawings.</li><li><strong>Rendering Edition:</strong> Enables citizens to express their design suggestions through cross-modal editing interfaces. Users can make modifications using natural interactions (text descriptions, sketches, or direct manipulation) without requiring technical drawing skills. The system translates these intuitive edits into design changes that are reflected in the visualizations.</li><li><strong>Viewpoint Visualization:</strong> Compiles and visualizes design contributions from multiple participants using provenance visualization techniques. This allows stakeholders to see the range of citizen perspectives and identify common themes or divergent preferences, facilitating more informed decision-making in the design process.</li></ul>
results: >
  <p>Our preliminary user studies with citizen participants demonstrate that ManyViews significantly improves engagement in urban design processes:</p><ul><li>Participants reported <strong>higher confidence</strong> in understanding design proposals when viewing AI-generated human-perspective renderings compared to traditional architectural drawings.</li><li>The cross-modal editing interface enabled non-experts to successfully communicate design suggestions that were previously difficult to express.</li><li>The provenance visualization effectively presented diverse citizen viewpoints, helping design teams identify consensus and areas of disagreement.</li></ul><p>The tool shows promise for enabling more inclusive and participatory urban design processes, particularly in contexts where citizen input has traditionally been limited by technical barriers.</p>

This project addresses a core barrier in public participation: non-experts struggle to build spatial understanding from professional design drawings and articulate design modification. Through <u>diffusion-based view generation</u>, <u>multimodal editing</u>, and <u>provenance visualization</u>, it enables non-experts to comprehend, reshape, and collectively reflect on urban design proposals.
